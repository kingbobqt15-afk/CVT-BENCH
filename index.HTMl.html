Know the parts

A short glossary before you start swapping parts on the bench.

WEIGHT

Roller / Flyweight

Sits in the movable face's ramps. Centrifugal force pushes it outward as RPM climbs, closing the pulley and forcing the belt up — heavier rollers close it sooner, lighter rollers let RPM climb higher first.

SPRING

CENTER SPRING

Pushes back against the rollers, resisting the pulley closing. A stiffer (higher RPM-rated) spring holds the ratio down longer and raises the RPM the engine sits at.

SPRING

CLUTCH SPRING

Controls the RPM at which the clutch shoes engage the bell and the bike starts moving. Higher rating means a later, harder engagement.

"Arangkada" is Filipino tuner slang for a setup tuned for a hard, fast launch off the line — common on jeepney and scooter tuning culture — as opposed to a setup tuned for a higher top speed.

This bench uses deterministic reference calculations for tuning guidance. It doesn't replace a proper road test or dyno run, and real results vary by bike model, belt condition, and riding style. Always test conservatively and re-check after any CVT change.
